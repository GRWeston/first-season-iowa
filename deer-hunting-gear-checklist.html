What Do I Need to Deer Hunt in Iowa?

You don’t need a truck full of gear for your first season. Here’s a checklist of what you actually need, what’s nice to have, and what can wait until year two.

By Walt Kessler. Checked against what he packed for his first hunt in 2004, minus everything he never used.

Paperwork first

  • Hunter education certificate, or an apprentice license
  • Iowa hunting license and habitat fee
  • A deer tag for your season
  • Written permission or a map, if you’re hunting private or public land you don’t know well

Not sure which license you need? Start with the license guide.

Your weapon depends on your season

Iowa sets different legal equipment for different seasons. Bows are used in archery season. Shotguns firing slugs, muzzleloaders, and certain other firearms are allowed in the gun seasons. Before you buy anything, check which weapons are legal for the season you’re hunting.

Practice before season. Whatever you hunt with, spend time at a range until you can hit a paper plate consistently at the distance you expect to shoot. A clean, ethical shot is your responsibility.

Must-have gear

  • Solid blaze orange clothing during the firearm seasons, such as a hat, vest, or coat
  • Warm layers you can add and remove, plus a waterproof outer layer
  • Insulated, quiet boots. December in Iowa is cold, and sitting still makes it colder
  • A sharp knife for field dressing
  • Disposable gloves and a few large zip bags
  • A headlamp or flashlight, since you’ll walk in before sunrise and out after sunset
  • A pen or marker for filling out your tag
  • A charged phone for reporting your harvest

Nice to have

  • Binoculars for spotting deer at the edge of a field
  • A seat cushion or small folding stool
  • Hand warmers
  • Rope or a drag strap to move your deer

What can wait

Tree stands, trail cameras, scent sprays, and calls all have their place, but none of them are required to fill a tag. Many beginners take their first deer sitting against a tree on the ground. Learn the basics first, then add gear that solves a problem you actually ran into.
